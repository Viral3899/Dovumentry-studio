STEP 6 — RENAME, DOWNLOAD, UPLOAD
After ALL images are generated:
1. Rename sequentially: 1.jpeg, 2.jpeg, 3.jpeg…
2. Download/export the project image set.
3. Return to Documentary Studio → Image Bay → upload all images.

The project uses 1 image per 5 seconds of audio, so the required total is CEILING(audio_seconds / 5).
